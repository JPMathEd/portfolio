  <div class="container approach-grid">
    <div class="approach-intro"><p class="eyebrow">My approach</p><h2 id="approach-title">Experience first.<br><em>Formalize later.</em></h2><p>I design opportunities for learners to explore, question, and build intuition before introducing formal ideas. Technology has a place when it helps that learning happen.</p></div>
    <div>
      <div class="principle"><span class="index" aria-hidden="true">01</span><div><h3>Begin with a real problem.</h3><p>Connect the learning to something students or educators already recognize as meaningful.</p></div></div>
      <div class="principle"><span class="index" aria-hidden="true">02</span><div><h3>Make the path accessible.</h3><p>Use clear structure, purposeful scaffolding, and multiple ways to engage without lowering the intellectual challenge.</p></div></div>
      <div class="principle"><span class="index" aria-hidden="true">03</span><div><h3>Use evidence to keep improving.</h3><p>Examine what learners produce, listen to their experiences, and revise the design—not just the explanation.</p></div></div>
    </div>
