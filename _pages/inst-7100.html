Introduction

I’m Joshua Plummer, a mathematics lecturer at Auburn University at Montgomery and an Ed.D. student in Instructional Systems and Learning Sciences. My work for INST 7100 connects adult learning theory with practical professional development for educators.

The toolkit below starts with problems mathematics faculty encounter in their own courses. I designed it to respect their experience, offer meaningful choices, and support changes they can put into practice.

Adult learning toolkit · 2026

Accessible & Rigorous

I designed three professional learning sessions on assessment redesign, accessible Canvas pages, and resources for students who miss class. The toolkit also includes coaching guidance, working tools, and a plan for continued professional learning.

I piloted a one-to-one adaptation of Your Page, Rebuilt on July 17, 2026. That experience informed a facilitation revision; it does not establish the effectiveness of all three sessions or an improvement in student outcomes.
